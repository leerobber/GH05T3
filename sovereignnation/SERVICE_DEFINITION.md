# SovereignNation — Service Definition

## What We Sell
Private AI installed on your hardware. Your data never leaves your building.
Same capability as ChatGPT. Zero per-query cost after setup. Zero cloud dependency.

## Service Tiers

### Tier 1 — Starter ($500 one-time)
**Best for:** Solo practitioners, 1-3 staff

**What's included:**
- Installation of Ollama + 2 sovereign agents of your choice on 1 machine
- Recommended agents: ORACLE (research/Q&A) + FORGE (drafting/writing)
- Private web interface accessible from any browser on your network
- 1 hour of onboarding and staff training (remote via Zoom)
- 30-day email support

**Client hardware requirement:**
- Windows 10/11 or macOS 12+
- 16GB RAM minimum (8GB will work for 1 agent at a time)
- 50GB free disk space
- No GPU required

**Setup time:** 2-4 hours (remote install via AnyDesk/TeamViewer)

## What We Do NOT Do
- We do not host your data on any cloud server
- We do not have access to your conversations after setup
- We do not share your data with any third party
- We do not require an internet connection after initial setup

---

## Compliance Notes
- **HIPAA:** No PHI leaves the building. No Business Associate Agreement needed (data stays local).
- **Attorney-client privilege:** All conversations remain on client hardware.
- **GDPR/CCPA:** No data processing agreement needed — data never transmitted.
- **FINRA/SEC:** Client financial data not shared with any third party.

*Note: SovereignNation does not provide legal or compliance advice. Clients should confirm with their own counsel.*
