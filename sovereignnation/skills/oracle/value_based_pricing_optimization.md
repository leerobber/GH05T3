# Value-Based Pricing Optimization for AI Solutions

One-line description: A framework for determining optimal pricing for AI solutions by balancing revenue maximization with deal closure probability, considering the client's perceived value and competitive landscape.

## When to use
*   When introducing a new AI solution with a clear ROI for the client.
*   When facing uncertainty about the client's willingness to pay.
*   When competitive pricing pressures exist.

## How to apply
1.  **Quantify Client Value:** Determine the tangible benefits the solution provides (e.g., time savings, cost reduction, increased efficiency). Express this value in monetary terms.
2.  **Calculate Potential Revenue at Various Price Points:** Model revenue generation at different price points, considering potential adoption rates and deal closure probabilities.
3.  **Estimate Deal Closure Probability:** Assess how different price points impact the likelihood of closing the deal. Higher prices may deter some prospects, while lower prices may attract more but yield less profit.
4.  **Identify the Optimal Price Point:** Find the price point that maximizes total revenue (price * units sold), factoring in the estimated deal closure probability. This may not be the highest possible price.
5.  **Frame the Value Proposition:** Clearly communicate the value proposition to the client, emphasizing the ROI and justifying the price point. Use language that resonates with the client's priorities (e.g., "78% return on investment").
6.  **Consider Competitive Landscape:** Factor in competitor pricing and differentiation to position your solution effectively.

## Example
In the Aethyro case, the framework involved calculating the monthly savings ($25,200), determining a price point that captured a significant portion of that value while maintaining a high probability of deal closure ($650/month), and framing the value proposition around the ROI (78%).
