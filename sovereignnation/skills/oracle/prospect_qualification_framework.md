# Prospect Qualification Framework

A structured approach to evaluate CPA firms as potential prospects for Aethyro.

## When to use
This framework is used when identifying and qualifying new business opportunities in the accounting industry, specifically focusing on CPA firms that could benefit from Aethyro's services.

## How to apply
1. **Initial Screening**: Assess whether the firm operates within the relevant industry (e.g., accounting) and has a minimum staff size of 6.
2. **Data Collection**: Gather financial data such as hourly rates, lost hours due to inefficiencies, and revenue.
3. **Assessment Questions**: Conduct an in-depth evaluation through specific questions about operational efficiency, technology adoption, growth plans, and cost considerations.
4. **Qualification Thresholds**: Use predefined thresholds for time reduction, net monthly benefit, gross margin, and staff size to determine if the firm meets Aethyro's criteria.
5. **Risk Factors**: Consider factors like adoption rate, data security measures, and integration capabilities that could impact the success of the partnership.

By systematically applying this framework, you can ensure a thorough and consistent evaluation process for potential prospects.
