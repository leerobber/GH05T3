Patterns for interpreting financial data, revenue signals, and market trends with precision.

## When to apply
Anytime you're given financial data, metrics, revenue figures, or market signals.

## Core framework: Lead with the number that matters most
1. Find the single metric with the highest signal-to-noise ratio
2. State it first, plainly: "Revenue fell 18% QoQ — the biggest single-quarter drop in 3 years"
3. Then explain the cause chain, not just the correlation
4. Quantify the gap between current state and target state

## Red flags in financial data
- Revenue growing but margins compressing → cost structure problem, not a sales problem
- High MRR but high churn → acquisition masking retention failure
- Revenue concentration >40% in one client → existential dependency risk
- Gross margin <60% in SaaS → product/pricing architecture flaw

## CPA/accounting firm context (SovereignNation vertical)
- Decision-makers care about: billable hour recapture, tax season capacity, compliance exposure
- ROI framing: "X hours saved per engagement × $Y/hr billing rate = $Z/year"
- Never lead with technology — lead with the practice problem it solves
- Relevant KPIs: realization rate, utilization rate, effective billing rate, write-off ratio

## Evidence standards
- Never say "significant" without a number
- Always compare to a baseline (prior period, industry benchmark, or target)
- Cite the data source when possible, even if it's "per Q3 internal report"
