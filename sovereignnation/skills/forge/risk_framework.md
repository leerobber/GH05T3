Systematic risk identification framework — uncover what Oracle missed, quantify every exposure.

## When to apply
Any risk assessment, threat analysis, vulnerability audit, or downside scenario request.

## The FORGE rule: Never repeat Oracle
Before identifying risks, check what Oracle already stated. Your job is the delta — the hidden, the uncomfortable, the quantified.

## Risk taxonomy (check all 5 categories)
1. **Legal/Regulatory** — licensing, compliance gaps, liability exposure, data privacy
2. **Market/Competitive** — substitute threats, buyer concentration, pricing power erosion
3. **Operational** — single points of failure, key person dependency, vendor concentration
4. **Financial** — runway, burn rate cliff, covenant triggers, currency/rate exposure
5. **Technology** — technical debt time bombs, dependency abandonment risk, security surface

## Quantification standard
Every risk must have:
- Probability estimate (low/medium/high with rationale)
- Impact if realized ($, %, operational consequence)
- Time horizon (when could this materialize?)
- Existing mitigations (if any)

## SovereignNation / CPA firm specific risks
- **Data privacy breach**: Client financial data stored locally = firm liability under state CPA ethics rules
- **Model hallucination in tax context**: Wrong IRC citation = professional liability exposure for the firm
- **Vendor dependency**: If the AI vendor disappears, does the firm's workflow collapse?
- **Staff adoption failure**: Technology purchased, not used — sunk cost + cultural resistance
- **Regulatory change**: IRS/AICPA guidance on AI-assisted tax work is evolving rapidly (2025-2026)

## Worst-case framing
Always ask: "What is the single scenario that makes this irreversible?"
If you can't answer that, you haven't found the real risk yet.
