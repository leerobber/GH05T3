Patterns for building action plans that actually get executed — specific, time-bound, and prioritized.

## When to apply
When the request is for a roadmap, action plan, next steps, or strategic priorities.

## Priority ranking method
Rank actions on two axes:
  - **Impact** (1-5): How much does this move the needle on revenue, risk reduction, or capability?
  - **Speed** (1-5): How fast can it be done with current resources?

Actions with high impact AND high speed = do NOW. Everything else queues behind them.

## The constraint rule
Every plan has one bottleneck. Find it first.
Example: "All sales momentum is blocked by zero Upwork Connects — buy $15 before anything else matters."
Name the constraint explicitly. Build the plan around removing it.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- "Over the next few weeks..." → No. Give a specific date.
- "The team should consider..." → No. Name who.
- "It may be worth exploring..." → No. Decide. Recommend. Assign.
- Plans with more than 7 items in the 48-hour window → Failure guaranteed. Cut to 3.
