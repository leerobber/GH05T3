FAQ

Common Questions

What hardware do I need? +
A modern workstation or server with at least 16GB RAM and a dedicated GPU (8GB+ VRAM) gives the best performance — typically a gaming PC or a business workstation made in the last 3 years. We assess your existing hardware on the intro call and let you know if any upgrades are needed before deployment. Many accounting firms already have suitable hardware on-site.
How long does installation take? +
The full deployment takes approximately 2 hours via remote access (we use a temporary screen-share session you initiate). We install all modules, run verification tests on your hardware, and walk your staff through the workflow during a 30-minute onboarding call. On-site installation is also available for firms that prefer it.
Is this HIPAA or SOC2 compliant? +
The architecture is designed to support compliance requirements: all data processing occurs on your hardware, there are no third-party API calls, and no client data is transmitted externally. We provide a technical data-flow document suitable for your compliance auditor or IT security review. Formal HIPAA BAA and SOC2 certification are available on the Enterprise plan.
What if the AI gives incorrect tax advice? +
SovereignNation is a research and drafting tool — the same way you'd use a junior associate to pull IRC sections and draft correspondence. The output should be reviewed and signed off by a licensed CPA or tax professional before client delivery. AXIOM cites all source materials so your team can verify every claim. This is not a replacement for professional judgment — it's a force multiplier for the research phase.
Can I customize the agents for my specific practice area? +
Yes. Every module has an editable system prompt that controls its behavior, tone, and specialization. On the Professional plan, we configure these during setup based on your practice area (e.g., specializing AXIOM in real estate tax, estate planning, or small business advisory). On the Enterprise plan, agents can be fine-tuned on your firm's own documents, templates, and historical work product.
What does the $299/month cover after setup? +
The monthly fee covers model updates as we improve and fine-tune the specialist modules, access to new modules as we release them, priority support response (same business day), monthly check-in calls, and the right to deploy to additional workstations at your firm without extra per-seat fees. There are no usage caps — your staff can run unlimited queries.
