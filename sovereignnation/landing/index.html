FAQ

Common Questions

What hardware do I need? +
A modern workstation or server with at least 16GB RAM and a dedicated GPU (8GB+ VRAM recommended) gives the best performance — typically a gaming PC or business workstation made in the last 3–4 years. We assess your existing hardware during the intro call and let you know if any upgrades are needed before deployment. Most accounting firms already have suitable hardware on-site. We can also advise on low-cost hardware upgrades if needed.
How long does installation take? +
The full deployment takes approximately 2 hours via remote access (you initiate a screen-share session). We install all modules, run verification tests on your hardware, configure system prompts for your practice area, and walk your staff through the workflow. On-site installation is also available for firms that prefer it. Most firms are running live queries the same day.
Is this HIPAA or SOC2 compliant? +
The architecture is designed to support compliance requirements: all processing occurs on your hardware, there are no third-party API calls, and no client data is transmitted externally. We provide a technical data-flow document suitable for compliance auditor review. Formal HIPAA BAA and SOC2 certification packages are available on the Enterprise plan.
What if the AI gives incorrect tax advice? +
SovereignNation is a research and drafting tool — the same role a junior associate plays when pulling IRC sections and drafting correspondence. The output should be reviewed by a licensed CPA or tax professional before client delivery. AXIOM cites all source materials so your team can verify every claim. This is a force multiplier for the research phase, not a replacement for professional judgment.
Can I customize agents for my specific practice area? +
Yes. Every module has an editable system prompt controlling behavior, tone, and specialization. On the Professional plan, we configure these during setup based on your practice area — whether that's real estate tax, estate planning, small business advisory, or S-Corp structuring. On the Enterprise plan, agents can be fine-tuned on your firm's own documents, templates, and historical work product.
What does the $299/month cover after setup? +
The monthly fee covers model updates as we improve and fine-tune the specialist modules, access to new modules as they release, same-business-day priority support response, monthly check-in calls, and the right to deploy to additional workstations at your firm without extra per-seat fees. There are no usage caps — your staff can run unlimited queries every day.
What happens to my data if I cancel? +
Your data never left your premises in the first place — so cancellation has no data implications. All models, logs, and configurations remain on your hardware and are yours to keep. We can provide an uninstall script to cleanly remove the SovereignNation software layer, or you can simply stop using it. No data deletion requests, no data recovery worries.
